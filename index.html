Generations of Flavour

Our Heritage Since 1905

More than a century of unhurried Goan hospitality, heirloom recipes, and community connection in Vasco da Gama.

1905

The Humble Beginning

Established in 1905 near the bustling port of Vasco da Gama, Hotel Anantashram began as a welcoming haven serving wholesome Goan meals to seafaring merchants, railway travellers, and local families.

4G

Generational Guardians

Through four generations of family stewardship, the core masala recipes, slow woodfire techniques, and devotion to fresh catch have remained untouched by industrial shortcuts or commercial trends.

5K+

A Living Landmark

Today, with over 5,595 verified Google reviews and diners spanning four generations, Anantashram stands proudly as one of Goa's enduring culinary monuments to authentic coastal dining.

Privacy Policy

1. Information You Share With Us

When reserving a table by phone or WhatsApp, we collect only your name, phone number, party size, dining time, and dietary preferences.

2. How We Use It

Your details are used solely to confirm and manage your table reservation and prepare for dietary requests. We never send unsolicited spam or marketing broadcasts.

3. No Financial Data Collected

We do not process or store any credit card, debit card, or bank details on this website. All bills are settled in person at the restaurant upon dining.

4. Guest Information Requests

You may request the deletion or correction of your booking details at any time by calling our reservations line at 077448 18888.
