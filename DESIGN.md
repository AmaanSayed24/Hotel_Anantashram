## Brand & Style

This design system embodies the warmth, richness, and timeless grace of traditional Goan coastal hospitality blended with refined high-end dining. It pays homage to ancestral culinary mastery, coastal breezes, hand-ground spices, and generational recipes. 

The aesthetic is rooted in **Warm Coastal Elegance** with touches of editorial luxury. It avoids kitschy beach motifs, leaning instead into Portuguese-Goan architectural grandeur: polished laterite stonework, antique teakwood finishes, hand-cast brass ware, and atmospheric golden-hour illumination. The interface should feel like stepping into a sun-dappled heritage villa—generous, fragrant, deeply comforting, yet meticulously curated and prestigious.

Key emotive goals:
- **Heritage & Trust:** Evoking the quiet pride of a legendary kitchen known for uncompromising authenticity.
- **Sensory Indulgence:** Inspiring hunger through warm coconut undertones, sun-ripened chili reds, and lustrous spice gold.
- **Gracious Hospitality:** Providing an effortless, unhurried digital experience for reserving tables, exploring heirloom recipes, or ordering curated thalis.

## Colors

The palette is derived directly from the landscape and culinary heritage of coastal Goa:

- **Primary (`#9E3D24` - Terracotta Spice):** A rich, clay-baked red reminiscent of Goan laterite walls, sun-dried Byadgi chilies, and clay-pot fish curries. Used for high-impact brand accents, primary CTAs, and active navigation indicators.
- **Secondary (`#1B2A4A` - Oceanic Abyssal Navy):** The deep, tranquil shade of the Arabian Sea at dusk. Delivers dignified structure, grounding text elements, headers, and evening dining motifs.
- **Tertiary (`#C8933E` - Burnished Brass & Spice Gold):** Inspired by polished brass urlis, whole turmeric, and flickering candlelight. Serves as a decorative framing accent, badge indicator, and luxury signifier for heritage highlights.
- **Neutral (`#F9F6F0` - Warm Coconut Cream):** A soft, natural off-white evoking grated fresh coconut flesh and lime-washed walls. Forms the canvas across light-mode viewports to maintain warmth and avoid sterile digital glare.

### Supporting Functional Roles:
- **Surface Elevation (`#FFFFFF`):** Reserved for elevated dish cards, modal sheets, and reservation forms.
- **Deep Muted Olive (`#3B4E38`):** Used subtly for vegetarian tags and fresh herb/sourcing markers.
- **Text Primary (`#191C21`):** Deep charcoal navy for optimal contrast and effortless legibility.
- **Text Muted (`#626875`):** Soft stone slate for supporting metadata, preparation times, and dish origin notes.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy pairs the regal character of `Playfair Display` with the crisp legibility of `Plus Jakarta Sans`.

- **Headlines & Editorial Statements:** Set in `Playfair Display`. It brings literary gravitas, reminiscent of vintage handwritten menus, vintage Portuguese signboards, and heritage dining institutions. Large titles should utilize tighter negative letter spacing and delicate italicization for culinary terms (e.g., *Tisryo Sukkem*, *Xacuti*).
- **Body & Operational Elements:** Set in `Plus Jakarta Sans`. Its friendly geometric proportions and open apertures offer exceptional clarity when scanning complex seafood menus, pricing structures, allergen tags, and reservation forms.
- **Labels & Micro-copy:** Uppercase treatments with wider tracking (`0.06em` to `0.08em`) are strictly reserved for categorization tags (e.g., "CATCH OF THE DAY", "HERITAGE RECIPE", "CHEF'S SIGNATURE").

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon an expansive, balanced fluid grid that mirrors architectural pacing. High-end culinary experiences require unhurried visual breathing room.

- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12-column grid with `4rem` outer canvas margins and `1.5rem` gutters. Maximum content container width is capped at `1280px` for optimal reading flow, with edge-to-edge full-bleed bands reserved for atmospheric imagery and heritage storytelling sections.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8-column grid with `2rem` outer margins and `1.25rem` gutters. Menu cards reflow into 2-column clusters.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout with `1.25rem` outer margins and `1rem` gutters. Horizontal swipe carousels are leveraged for signature categories (e.g., Fish Thalis, Coastal Starters) to preserve vertical scrolling rhythm.

### Spacing Philosophy:
- Use generous vertical spacing (`space-xl` and higher multiples) between distinct dining chapters (Heritage Story vs. Table Booking) to reinforce an unhurried, luxurious narrative.
- Group culinary attributes tightly: Dish name, konkani nomenclature, ingredients, and pricing are bound via `space-xs` and `space-sm`.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through **ambient warm shadows** paired with **tonal layers** to emulate soft candlelit tables rather than stark digital planes.

- **Surface Tiers:**
  - **Base Canvas:** Neutral Warm Coconut Cream (`#F9F6F0`).
  - **Mid-Tier (Cards & Story Sections):** Pure Surface White (`#FFFFFF`) or Warm Amber-tinted Wash (`#F3ECE1`).
  - **High-Tier Floating Elements (Booking Bar, Sticky Cart, Modals):** Crisp white with golden-tinted ambient diffusion.
- **Shadow Profiles:**
  - **Resting Elements (Menu Cards, Category Tiles):** `0px 4px 20px -2px rgba(27, 42, 74, 0.05), 0px 2px 6px -1px rgba(158, 61, 36, 0.03)`
  - **Hover / Interactive Elevation:** `0px 12px 32px -4px rgba(27, 42, 74, 0.09), 0px 4px 12px -2px rgba(200, 147, 62, 0.12)`
  - **Floating Overlays & Sticky Nav:** `0px 16px 40px -6px rgba(27, 42, 74, 0.14)`
- **Linework:** Borders are featherlight (`1px`) in muted tones (`rgba(27, 42, 74, 0.08)` or brass-tinted `rgba(200, 147, 62, 0.25)`), creating subtle delineation without harsh graphic containment.

## Shapes

The interface embraces a **Soft architectural geometry** (`roundedness: 1`). It honors classic Portuguese archways and handcrafted wooden tabletops with refined, gentle corners rather than hyper-rounded or bubble-like shapes.

- Standard interactive controls (buttons, input fields, dropdowns) carry a restrained `0.25rem` (4px) corner radius.
- Cards, reservation panels, and imagery containers employ `rounded-lg` (`0.5rem` / 8px).
- Specialized visual elements—such as heritage archival photographs, chef portraits, and spice vignettes—may utilize classic arched top borders (`border-top-left-radius: 9999px; border-top-right-radius: 9999px`) to echo Portuguese colonial window frames and coastal veranda arches.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive CTAs
- **Primary CTA ("Reserve a Table", "Order Thali"):** Solid Terracotta (`#9E3D24`) background with clean white typography, `0.25rem` radius, subtle inner warmth, and smooth elevation shift on hover.
- **Secondary / Heritage Button:** Oceanic Navy (`#1B2A4A`) fill or transparent background with a 1.5px Burnished Gold (`#C8933E`) border and navy text.
- **Text Action:** Playfair serif or bold uppercase sans with a persistent gold underline that expands outward on hover.

### Culinary Cards & Thali Showcases
- **Structure:** Clean elevated white cards with high-aspect culinary photography (4:3 or 1:1), delicate 1px border (`rgba(200, 147, 62, 0.2)`), and integrated spice tags.
- **Specialty Badges:** Floating pill tags over images with a semi-opaque navy or cream backdrop indicating "Catch of the Day", "Legendary Special", or "Kokum Infused".
- **Pricing & Portions:** Distinct tabular numbers in Oceanic Navy with spice gold currency markers.

### Category Chips & Dietary Filters
- **Dormant:** Coconut cream background with subtle border, muted stone slate text.
- **Active:** Deep oceanic navy background with white text and gold micro-accent, or full Terracotta fill for dietary toggles (e.g., "Sea Catch", "Traditional Thalis", "Claypot Biryani", "Pure Veg").

### Online Reservation Engine & Date Picker
- **Form Surface:** Warm linen card with dual-column inputs.
- **Inputs:** Crisp white background with `0.25rem` border, transitioning to a focused state with a `1.5px` spice-gold glow and deep navy placeholder labels.
- **Guest Selector & Time Slots:** Segmented pills with clear visual feedback for prime dining hours and sunset coastal seatings.

### Heritage Story & Testimonial Panels
- **Layout:** Generous alternating editorial split-screen with archive photos, vintage sepia undertones, and pulled quotes styled in large, italicized `Playfair Display`.
- **Review Stars & Verification:** Burnished brass star glyphs accented with guest city and visit context (e.g., "Family Feast", "Culinary Traveler").
