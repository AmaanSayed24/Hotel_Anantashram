Privacy Policy

1. Information You Share With Us

When you reserve a table by phone or WhatsApp, we collect only what is needed to hold your table — your name, contact number, party size, preferred dining time, and any dietary notes you choose to share.

2. How We Use It

Your details are used solely to confirm and manage your reservation, prepare for dietary requirements, and contact you if your table or today's fresh catch availability changes. We do not send marketing messages.

3. No Card or Payment Data

We never collect, process, or store card or bank details. All payments are settled in person at the restaurant on the day of your visit.

4. Call & Chat Records

Call logs and WhatsApp conversations are retained only for as long as necessary to service your booking, after which they are removed from our devices.

5. Cookies & Aggregate Data

We may review aggregated, non-identifying statistics such as Google review ratings and website visits to improve our service. We never sell, rent, or trade guest information with advertisers.

6. Your Choices

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your booking details at any time by calling our reservations line.

7. Contact Us
